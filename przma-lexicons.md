## **LEXICON ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW**

### **Universal Cultural Dimensions Framework**
Instead of fixed cultural frameworks, PRZMA uses configurable cultural dimensions that any community can customize:

1. **Communication Patterns**: High/low context, direct/indirect, storytelling traditions
2. **Social Structures**: Individual/collective, hierarchical/egalitarian, family systems
3. **Value Systems**: Achievement/relationship focus, time orientation, authority respect
4. **Spiritual/Philosophical**: Worldview, life purpose, moral authority, sacred practices
5. **Economic/Material**: Resource sharing, work orientation, success definitions
6. **Environmental/Place**: Land connection, climate wisdom, spatial relationships

### **Processing Integration**
- **7P Framework**: People, Process, Products, Places, Prices, Packages, Partnerships
- **PRESERVE Framework**: Presence, Relationships, Enablement, Stories, Execution, Resources, Value Creation, Excellence
- **Age Range**: 2-99 years with developmental appropriateness
- **Mixed Heritage**: Support for multiple cultural influences simultaneously
